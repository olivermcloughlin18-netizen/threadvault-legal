Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

1. Overview

ThreadVault Automator ("the App") is a personal desktop application. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

2. Data We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. The App operates entirely on your local machine.

The only external services the App communicates with are:

  • TikTok Content Posting API — to publish videos to your TikTok account on your behalf.
  • ElevenLabs API — to generate text-to-speech audio from post content.
  • OpenAI Whisper API — to generate word-level timestamps for captions.
  • Reddit public JSON API — to fetch publicly available post content (no account required).

3. TikTok Data

When you connect your TikTok account, the App stores an access token locally on your device. This token is used solely to post videos to your TikTok account. We do not read, store, or share your TikTok profile data, followers, messages, or any other account information.

4. Data Storage

All data (videos, audio, captions, job history) is stored locally on your device. No data is uploaded to or stored on our servers because we operate no servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Your use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's Privacy Policy. Your use of ElevenLabs and OpenAI is subject to their respective privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when changes are made.
